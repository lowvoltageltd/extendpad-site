Tethered iPad display

Your iPad,as a wired second screen.

A tethered, Sidecar-style display for iPads, old and new. Plug in a cable and your Mac gets an extra screen, with touch. No Wi-Fi, no account, and completely free.

Use the hardware you already own

That drawer iPad still has a great screen. 2nd Display puts it to work, without a monthly fee.

Wired and fast

Video and touch travel over the cable, so there's no Wi-Fi to set up and nothing to compete with on a busy network.

Old iPads welcome

Runs on anything with iOS 15 or later, including the iPad mini 4 and iPad Air 2 that Apple's own Sidecar leaves out.

Touch included

Tap and drag on the iPad to click and move things on the Mac.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no servers. Your screen goes down the cable to your iPad and nowhere else.

Set up in a minute

Install once, then it's just plug in and go.

  1. Install both appsDownload the Mac app and drag it to Applications. Install 2nd Display on your iPad from the App Store.
  2. Allow two permissionsOn the Mac, allow Screen Recording (to capture the extra display) and Accessibility (so iPad touches can click). macOS asks the first time.
  3. Plug in and openConnect the iPad with its cable and open 2nd Display on it. A new display appears in System Settings → Displays. Arrange it like any monitor.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Both the Mac app and the iPad app are free, with no subscription, no ads and no in-app purchases.

Why do I need a Mac app as well?

The Mac app creates the extra display and sends it to the iPad. iOS apps can't do that on their own, so the Mac side is a small menu-bar app you download here.

Does it work over Wi-Fi?

No, it's deliberately wired. That keeps it simple, reliable and independent of your network.

Why does the Mac ask for Screen Recording and Accessibility?

Screen Recording lets 2nd Display capture the extra display it creates (only that display, not your main screen). Accessibility lets it turn your iPad touches into mouse clicks. If you don't need touch, you can skip Accessibility.

Is it laggy?

It's tuned for responsiveness. Older iPads have less decoding power, so on a very old model you may get smoother results at a lower frame rate, which is a one-line setting in the source.

Touch or keyboard stopped working after an update?

macOS can quietly stop honouring a permission when an app is updated, even though the switch still looks on. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, select 2nd Display and click − to remove it, then + to add it again (or switch it off and on), and relaunch 2nd Display. If the picture doesn't appear, do the same under Screen Recording. The 2nd Display menu shows a warning, with a button to the right page, whenever a permission isn't working.

Does it work with Windows?

Not at the moment. 2nd Display is Mac + iPad.

Is it affiliated with Apple?

No. Sidecar is an Apple feature and a trademark of Apple Inc.; 2nd Display is an independent app that does something similar for iPads Sidecar doesn't cover.
