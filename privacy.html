Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

ExtendPad (the "iPad app" and the "Mac app") is published by Low Voltage Ltd. The short version: we don't collect your data.

What ExtendPad does with your screen

The Mac app creates an extra virtual display, captures only that display, compresses it and sends it over the USB cable to the iPad app, which shows it. Touches on the iPad are sent back over the same cable. This data goes directly between your two devices. It is not sent over the internet, and it is not stored.

What we collect

Nothing. ExtendPad has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network requests to any server operated by us or anyone else.

Permissions on the Mac

  • Screen Recording: used only to capture the extra display ExtendPad creates, so it can be shown on your iPad.
  • Accessibility: used only to turn touches on the iPad into mouse clicks and drags on that display.

Downloads

The Mac app is downloaded from GitHub Releases and the iPad app from the App Store. Those services have their own privacy policies and may log your download; we don't receive that information from them beyond what they show every publisher.

Children

ExtendPad collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
